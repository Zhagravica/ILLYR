Download ILLYR

Install in a minute

  1. Tap Download ILLYR. If Chrome asks, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file (ILLYR.apk) from the notification.
  3. Android asks to allow installs from your browser: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. Have ILLYR on your TV? Choose Copy from My TV and scan the QR code under the TV's Settings › Sync › Link a Phone.

ILLYR updates itself: when a new version is out, the app offers it on launch.
