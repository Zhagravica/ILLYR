Installing

ILLYR isn't sold through an app store, so your device asks once whether you trust it. Here's what to click.

  1. Download ILLYR-Setup.exe and open it.
  2. If Windows shows Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. (ILLYR isn't signed with a paid Microsoft certificate yet.)
  3. ILLYR installs and opens by itself, with shortcuts on the desktop and in the Start menu.
  4. Choose Copy from My TV and type the sync code from your TV's Settings › Sync, or add your provider.
Updates install themselves: when a new version is out, ILLYR downloads it and asks to restart.
  1. Download the .dmg for your Mac and open it.
  2. Drag ILLYR onto the Applications folder.
  3. Open ILLYR from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the developer: click Done.
  4. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to ILLYR, then confirm. From then on it opens normally.
  5. Choose Copy from My TV and type the sync code from your TV's Settings › Sync, or add your provider.
On a new version, ILLYR tells you and opens this page: download it and drag it to Applications again (choose Replace). Your profiles and history stay.
  1. Tap Download for Android. If Chrome asks, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open ILLYR.apk from the notification.
  3. Android asks to allow installs from your browser: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. Have ILLYR on your TV? Choose Copy from My TV and scan the QR code under the TV's Settings › Sync › Link a Phone.
Updates come through the app: when a new version is out, ILLYR offers it on launch.
ILLYR plays streams from the IPTV service you subscribe to; it doesn't provide any channels or content.
