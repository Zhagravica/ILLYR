# ILLYR – IPTV Player for Windows, Mac, Android and Samsung TV

ILLYR is a free IPTV player for Live TV, movies and series from your Xtream Codes or M3U provider.
It runs on Samsung TVs, Android phones, Windows PCs and Macs, with up to four profiles, free
subtitles and sync between all your devices.

**Download:** https://zhagravica.github.io/ILLYR/

- Windows 10 and 11: `ILLYR-Setup.exe`
- macOS (Apple silicon and Intel): `ILLYR-Mac-arm64.dmg` / `ILLYR-Mac-x64.dmg`
- Android 7+: `ILLYR.apk`

All files are on the [Releases](https://github.com/Zhagravica/ILLYR/releases/latest) page.

ILLYR plays streams from the IPTV service you subscribe to; it doesn't provide any channels or content.
